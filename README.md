# 🎯 Resumez: Enterprise AI Resume & Career Suite

An enterprise-grade platform designed for university students and early-career candidates to tailor their application materials specifically for target companies and locations using GPT.

## 🌟 Enterprise Features

1. **Focused 3-Step Wizard Flow**:
   - **Step 1: Candidate Context**: Upload/paste profile + target location preference.
   - **Step 2: Company Discovery**: Explore AI-matched companies with official website links (`🌐 Website ↗`), verified headquarters, office hubs, and work policy.
   - **Step 3: Enterprise Suite**: Comprehensive ATS diagnostic breakdown, dual document downloads, before/after diffs, and interview prep.

2. **Dual File Export (DOCX + Direct PDF)**:
   - 📄 **Jake's Resume Template**: 1-click download as either `.docx` or `.pdf` in strict 100% Black & White ATS standard.
   - ✉️ **Tailored Application Letter**: 1-click download as either `.docx` or `.pdf`.

3. **📊 4-Category ATS Diagnostic Breakdown**:
   - Tech Stack & Hard Skills Alignment
   - Google XYZ Formula & Quantifiable Impact Score
   - Single-Column ATS Format Compliance Rate
   - 1-Page Brevity & Word Density Score

4. **🔄 "Before vs. After" Bullet Point Transformations**:
   - Interactive cards showing the candidate's raw draft alongside the elevated Google XYZ bullet, highlighting the active verbs and metrics added.

5. **🎙️ AI Mock Interview Preparation**:
   - 3 Technical Architecture questions based on the projects highlighted in the tailored resume.
   - 2 Behavioral questions tied to the specific employer's culture and values (e.g. Stripe, Amazon, Google, Palantir) with STAR method answering strategies.

6. **💼 LinkedIn Profile Alignment Suite**:
   - Ready-to-copy LinkedIn Headline (keyword-optimized).
   - 3-sentence professional LinkedIn "About" bio connecting coursework to the employer's industry.

7. **Clean White & Blue UI (Zero Black Input Boxes)**:
   - Inputs are styled with crisp white backgrounds and soft blue borders, forced to Light mode via `.streamlit/config.toml`.
